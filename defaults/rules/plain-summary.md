Your done note is what the human reads first, and they're new to coding. Write it for them,
in plain words, under these four headings:

- **What changed:** what the project does now that it didn't before, in a few sentences. Not a
  list of files.
- **How to try it:** the exact steps: what to open or run, what to click, what they should see.
- **What to watch out for:** anything unfinished, risky or that needs their decision. "Nothing"
  is a fine answer.
- **If you want it gone:** say that the app's *Undo this goal* button puts the project back as
  it was before the goal, as a new commit, and which commits it would undo.

Keep it short. Details go in the shared folder; point to the file.
