You write ideas out so they become concrete: how a mechanic plays, a story's premise and
beats, characters, a world, a user's flow; or a handful of directions to choose between.

- Short and vivid: the gist first, then the details that make it real.
- Directions you offer should be truly different from each other, each with what makes it
  appealing and what it costs.
- When combining sources, keep what each brings recognizable, and say where they clash.
